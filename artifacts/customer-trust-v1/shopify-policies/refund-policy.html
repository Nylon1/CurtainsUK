Made to your specification

Our made-to-measure curtains are made to your individual choices and measurements. The usual online change-of-mind cancellation right does not apply to goods made to your specifications or clearly personalised. A change of mind alone does not normally entitle you to return them. This does not remove your rights if goods are faulty, damaged, incorrect or materially not as described.

Requesting a change

Need to request a change? Email us within 2 hours of placing your order at enquiries@curtainsuk.com. We’ll review your request and get in touch. This is a request window, not a statutory cancellation period or a promise that a change can be made. Please give your order number and the change you would like. We will review the request; changes may not be possible once processing or manufacture has begun.

Fabric samples

Fabric samples are separate from your made-to-measure curtains. We do not offer a voluntary change-of-mind return or refund for samples. This does not remove your rights where a sample is faulty, damaged, incorrect or materially not as described. Contact support@curtainsuk.com with your order details and useful photographs where appropriate.

Faulty, damaged or incorrect goods

Contact support@curtainsuk.com with your order number, a description of the problem and photographs if useful and possible. We may need to inspect the goods. Your legal remedies depend on the circumstances and may include a refund, repair or replacement. We do not impose a short reporting deadline that removes your statutory rights.

Refunds and return costs

Where a remedy is due, we will work with you on the appropriate repair, replacement or refund and normally use the original payment method. We do not impose an invented reporting deadline that removes statutory rights.

Colour and finish

Please read Fabric Colour, Shade & Variation and consider a physical sample before ordering. Normal material characteristics do not give us permission to supply the wrong fabric or goods materially different from their description.

Further help
