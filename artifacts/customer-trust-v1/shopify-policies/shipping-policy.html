Before ordering

Check the delivery area, charge and timing supplied for your order before payment. Samples and made-to-measure curtains are different order types and may travel separately.

Made-to-measure curtains

Your paid order goes through CurtainsUK review and approval for manufacture before workroom release. Payment alone does not mean your curtains are in production. Contact support@curtainsuk.com with your order reference for help.

Receiving your order

Check the delivery details before ordering. If goods arrive damaged, incorrect or incomplete, contact support@curtainsuk.com. Read Refunds & Returns; this guidance does not limit your legal rights.
