            - heading "A little guidance, room to experiment" [level=3]
            - article:
              - heading "Warm or cool?" [level=3]
              - heading "Warm colours" [level=4]
              - paragraph: Cream, warm beige, terracotta, blush and warm browns can make a room feel warmer and more inviting.
              - heading "Cool colours" [level=4]
              - paragraph: Blue, sage, blue-grey and cooler greens can create a calmer, fresher feel.
              - paragraph: Neutrals have undertones too. A beige may lean yellow, pink or grey, and these undertones can affect how it works with a curtain fabric.
            - article:
              - heading "Remember the floor" [level=3]
              - paragraph: Flooring is a large area of colour and can strongly affect a scheme.
              - list:
                - paragraph:
                  - strong: Light oak
                  - text: Often feels lighter and warmer.
                - paragraph:
                  - strong: Natural or medium oak
                  - text: A balanced, neutral base.
                - paragraph:
                  - strong: Dark wood
                  - text: Adds stronger contrast.
                - paragraph:
                  - strong: Grey floors
                  - text: Generally create a cooler base.
              - paragraph: Choose the closest visualiser floor tone to your existing floor, rather than trying to match it exactly on screen.
