## Approved operational wording available for insertion

**Contact:** Contact CurtainsUK at enquiries@curtainsuk.com. For a specialist project, quote your project reference when emailing photographs or drawings.

**Delivery:** Ordinary UK Mainland delivery is £12.95. Highlands & Islands and Northern Ireland delivery is £19.95. These amounts include VAT. Delivery for specialist or unusually large/heavy orders is confirmed after review. International delivery is unavailable for launch. The delivery amount is confirmed before checkout.

**Room images:** Adding a reference image is optional, with a maximum of one analysed image per consultation. The current service processes it privately and discards the raw image after analysis, sooner than the maximum 30-day retention period. There is no public image URL or raw-image archive. We do not use customer images for training without separate explicit permission. Original colour observations, confirmed palettes, corrections and consultation/recommendation events remain separate. Deletion enquiries go to enquiries@curtainsuk.com with the consultation reference.
