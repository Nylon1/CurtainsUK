## Considered and excluded

- Austin fabric-back image: does not teach fold, length or position.
- Other Austin colourways: duplicate instruction, unnecessary range-like presentation.
- Oakland downloaded image and generic wave living-room image: source/licence not sufficiently established.
- Additional Apex barn and airport lounge views: duplicate architecture lessons or overly commercial context.
- Pencil Newcastle installation image: cluttered installation scene; clearer labelled Apex source available.
- Generic CurtainsUK luxury room assets: real-photo provenance not established for this task.

## Evidence policy

All comparisons say they show different representative fabrics/rooms. Image cropping is presentational only; no recolouring, relighting or changing folds. Fullness is explanatory language, not selectable order data. Controlled same-fabric comparison is deferred to Phase 2. Image count may change if a clearer authorised example is supplied.
