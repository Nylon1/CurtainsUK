## Measure and compare

For each specimen, record the following. Use comparable camera positions and lighting for photographs, but judge the sewn product against its approved specification and usable appearance, not exact render folds:

- Finished width and height at the maximum spans; centre depth; top, side and lower edge bow; corner shape; seam width and closure position.
- Front, true side, three-quarter, back and sofa-resting photographs, with a ruler in at least one measurement image.
- Whether the existing insert and normal filling method produce a sound, softly filled cover with usable corners, seams and sofa contact. Differences in fullness or individual creases from the render are expected and do not by themselves call for new panels, extra fabric or a higher price.
- On the stripe, whether spacing remains visually consistent through the shoulder and thin sewn edge.
- On the directional fabric, whether both outward faces show the same motif at the same placement, upright when each face is viewed normally. Note any extra matching cut, waste or supplier minimum actually required.
- Time to cut, match, sew, fill and inspect, compared with the current standard making assumption. Record actual minutes rather than inferring a new rate from appearance.
