- Curved self-piping now has a conservative mathematical cloth plan. A 45 degree strip of length L and 40 mm width occupies a square envelope of side `(L + 40) / sqrt(2)` mm. Complete envelopes are placed without overlap after the main panels, including when a narrow width requires multiple strips.
