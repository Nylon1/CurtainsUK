## Pattern, insert and piping acceptance

Front and back must use the same identity/colourway and identical upright, unmirrored motif placement. Centre the chosen motif where reproducible. Record both physical cuts using verified repeats and direction, including extra matching cloth. Adjacent panels are not proof of matching. The bolster has a continuous body wrap and separately matching end panels.

Use a compatible standard insert; do not enlarge it to imitate rendered fullness. Confirm the supplier SKU, cover-size compatibility, actual insert dimensions and cost. The £5 standard allowance is settled only for confirmed compatible standard inserts. Round, bolster and large bed inserts need their own costs. New filled configurations require this evidence; cover-only configurations do not depend on inserts.

Piping proposals: 4 mm cord and approximately 40 mm strips. Confirm direction, joins, turning/attachment allowance and waste. Bias cutting may be necessary, especially for circles; the current calculator does not assert a verified bias yield. Such configurations remain blocked until the required construction/yield is supported and confirmed. Piping fabric is costed separately from complete making charges; no unverified strip consumption is silently charged.
