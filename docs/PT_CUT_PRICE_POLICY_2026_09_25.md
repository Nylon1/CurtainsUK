# Prestigious Cut Price policy

The owner confirmed that CurtainsUK uses Prestigious Textiles Cut Price ex VAT.
The exact owner-authorised batch selects only approved GBP `cut_trade_price`;
it does not derive, copy, or relabel Standard Price. Existing PT records with
only historical Standard evidence retain that evidence as a compatibility
fallback until their own Cut Price is supplied. The August 2026 supplier PDF
is never replaced with workbook Price or RRP.
