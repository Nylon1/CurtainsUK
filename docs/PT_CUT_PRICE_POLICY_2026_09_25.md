# Prestigious Cut Price policy

The owner confirmed Cut Price ex VAT for the owner-authorised August 2026 PDF
batch. That governed importer selects only approved GBP `cut_trade_price` and
does not derive, copy, or relabel Standard Price. Existing PT records retain
their established approved Standard-price selection. Cut-only evidence is
eligible only when emitted by the governed PDF importer, so historic Webtex or
earlier bulk Cut-only records cannot change public guides. The PDF is never
replaced with workbook Price or RRP.
