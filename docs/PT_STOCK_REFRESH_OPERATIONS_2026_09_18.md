# Prestigious Textiles stock refresh

The Production Webtex source is the authenticated Stock Enquiry collection search. The reader checks the current 3,185 non-discontinued Fabric Master PT SKUs by collection, then design and exact product code where needed. Only exact SKUs with numeric metre evidence enter the existing supplier-observation, approval and scoped daily-stock materialisation path. Extra collection results never expand the manifest. Conflicting quantities quarantine the SKU and stop promotion if proven coverage changes.

The approved routine runs every 72 hours, checked hourly by GitHub Actions. Stock evidence remains current for 96 hours. The worker logs a successful full run only after authentication, retrieval, exact validation, append, approval, scoped materialisation and verification. A failed job reports its stage and leaves existing observation timestamps unchanged. GitHub Actions failure notifications and `curtainsuk_private.supplier_sync_runs` provide operational history; the successful run row records expected and appended counts. PT and SDG share the existing 96-hour commercial policy but have separate supplier refresh jobs.

Repository Actions secrets `PT_WEBTEX_USERNAME` and `PT_WEBTEX_PASSWORD` hold the authorised trade-account login. The existing Supabase service secrets are reused for the same Fabric Master project. The Webtex session cookies are held in process memory for one run. No browser session token is persisted. Missing supplier results are not zero and are not re-dated.

The first full Production run on 18 September 2026 resolved 3,182/3,185: 2,827 at least 30m, 324 positive below 30m, and 31 explicit zero. The unresolved SKUs are `3622/282`, `7222/022` and `7866/012`. They received no new observation. Their earlier genuine snapshots remain in the audit history and may remain within the approved 96-hour commercial freshness window until expiry; absence from a new Webtex search alone does not invalidate a previously genuine quantity under the existing stock policy. Review that customer-facing nuance before claiming these three are immediately unavailable to sample.

Curtains require fresh available-now stock of at least 30m and enough metres for the calculated job. Samples require fresh positive available-now stock, without the 30m floor. No future/offsite metres are counted. PT stock changes do not rebuild HCI or change Shopify.
