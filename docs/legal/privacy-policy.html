Information we use

  • Contact and order information, including your name, email, delivery and billing details, products, selected fabrics, measurements, configuration, order history and payment status.
  • Messages and information you provide when asking for help, arranging a quote, or contacting us about an order or privacy request.
  • Optional consultation information, including your answers, design preferences, reference image, suggested and confirmed palettes, corrections, reactions, recommendations and sample or curtain journey events.
  • Technical information needed to operate and protect the website, including device and browser information, IP address, session identifiers, cookies and security or error logs.

Payment details are processed through the payment provider selected at checkout. Our curtain consultation does not need your card details. Please do not send payment details by email or include them in an uploaded image.

Why we use this information

We use information to respond to enquiries, provide consultations and quotes, calculate and fulfil your chosen curtain or sample order, process payments, arrange delivery, provide customer support and preserve your choices when you return.

We also use relevant records to protect the service, prevent fraud, investigate errors, assess recommendation quality and improve the customer experience. We use order and transaction records where needed to meet accounting and other legal obligations.

Our legal bases are taking steps at your request before a contract and performing that contract; compliance with legal obligations; and our legitimate interests in operating, securing and improving the service, subject to your rights. Where consent is required, including for non-essential cookies or separately permitted image-training use, we ask for it. You may withdraw consent without affecting the lawfulness of earlier processing.

Optional room and reference images

You may provide a maximum of one reference image per consultation. Uploading is optional: you can use the guided questions or browse fabrics without an image.

Your image is processed privately to help identify visible colours and design context for your consultation. It has no public image URL. You can keep, remove, add or move colours before confirming your palette. The original machine observation is kept separately from your confirmed palette.

Raw reference images are automatically deleted no later than 30 days after upload. The current service discards the raw image after analysis, so deletion currently happens sooner; it does not keep a raw-image archive. You may request earlier deletion by emailing enquiries@curtainsuk.com with your consultation reference. Please do not resend the image with your request.

Derived palette, recommendation and feedback records may be retained where required for service operation and improvement. These include your answers, original colour observation, confirmed palette, corrections, image hash, analysis version and journey events. An image hash is an identifier, not a viewable image.

We do not use customer-uploaded room or reference images for model training without separate explicit permission. Individual corrections and reactions do not automatically retrain or change our recommendation system. Please avoid including people, addresses, documents or other unnecessary personal information in images.

Who receives information

We use Shopify to operate the storefront and checkout, the payment providers available at checkout, and technical providers supporting hosting, data storage, private image analysis and communications. Relevant order and delivery information may be shared with those helping us make and deliver your order. These services receive information needed for their role. We may also disclose information where required by law or to protect legal rights.

Some service providers may process information outside the UK. The arrangements depend on the provider and service. You can contact us for information about the providers and transfer protections relevant to your information. Shopify explains its processing and international arrangements in its privacy policy.

Cookies and saved choices

The website uses cookies and browser storage for functions such as the basket, checkout, session security and remembering your consultation or selected fabric. Browser settings let you manage or remove stored information; doing so may clear saved choices or prevent parts of the service from working. Where non-essential cookies require consent, use the privacy choices presented on the website.

How long we keep information

The raw-image limit is set out above. Other information is retained only for as long as needed for its purpose. We consider whether a consultation or order is still active, whether you need to resume your choices, outstanding support or refund requests, service-quality and security investigations, and applicable accounting, legal and dispute requirements. Derived consultation records may be kept where needed to operate or improve the service; this does not extend raw-image retention or permit image training without separate permission.

Your choices and rights

Depending on the circumstances, you can ask to access, correct or erase your personal information, restrict or object to its use, or receive a portable copy. Contact enquiries@curtainsuk.com to make a request. We may need to verify your identity, and some information may need to be retained to meet legal obligations or resolve a claim.

HCI offers design suggestions. You can correct the palette, choose whether to follow a recommendation, or browse independently. Recommendations do not make decisions about your legal rights.

If you have a concern, please contact us using the details above. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office.

Changes to this notice

We may update this notice to reflect changes to the service or our obligations. The date above identifies this version. If we propose a new use that requires your consent, we will ask separately.
