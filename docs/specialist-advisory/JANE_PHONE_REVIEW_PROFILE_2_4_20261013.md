Please read each full conversation, then tell us which case numbers **work**, **need changes**, or are **uncertain**. For a case needing changes, explain what Jane missed, repeated or said at excessive length. ## Case 15: outside-product-range

**Review focus**

- No sound-isolation guarantee or unsupported numeric performance
- Suggest appropriate building/acoustic investigation without a forced sale

**Customer:** Can curtains completely stop road noise coming through my old windows?

**Jane:** No, curtains cannot completely stop road noise coming through old windows. Heavy, well-fitted curtains may soften some sound and reduce room echo, but they will not reliably block traffic noise, especially low engine rumble.

For a meaningful improvement, have a glazing specialist assess gaps, seals and whether secondary glazing would help. Curtains can complement that work, rather than replace it. Is the main disturbance tyre hiss or low rumbling from buses and lorries?

**Customer:** I need a guarantee before buying; I cannot afford to waste money.

**Jane:** I cannot guarantee that curtains will reduce your road noise enough, and with your budget at stake, I would not recommend buying them for that purpose.

Before spending, ask a glazing or acoustic specialist to assess where sound is entering through your old windows. Request a written proposal stating the expected noise reduction, its limitations and any guarantee. That gives you a firmer basis for deciding whether the cost is worthwhile.

**Customer:** What should I investigate before choosing any fabric?

**Jane:** Before choosing fabric, investigate where the noise enters: gaps around the frames, worn seals, the glazing itself or nearby vents. Ask a glazing or acoustic specialist which repairs or secondary glazing could help, with written evidence of expected performance and any guarantee’s conditions. Do not block ventilation.

Given your budget, establish whether window work is needed first. Only then consider fabric for privacy, light control and appearance, not guaranteed road-noise protection.
