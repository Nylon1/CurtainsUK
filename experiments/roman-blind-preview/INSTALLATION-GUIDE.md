# Roman installation guide (local preview)

Entry: `http://127.0.0.1:4318/install.html`. Not published to Shopify or Vercel.

Six illustrated stages: prepare, position, fix brackets, fit headrail, chain safety, test and dress. Uses the existing measuring-guide layout and links to measuring and visualiser pages. Top/face fixing is independent of recess type; the saved measuring choice only supplies the initial suggestion. Step links, Back/Next, keyboard focus and browser history work. Progress uses `curtainsuk-roman-install-v1`; changing mounting method clears the previous fitting checks. Completion requires all checks and the bracket match.

## Hardware scope

Owner confirmed Evans, not the exact delivered headrail/bracket. This preview explicitly illustrates manual Evaglide BSXL with universal swivel-lock bracket 910002. Users must match their fittings before the rail-fitting text is shown. Do not present this as instructions for every Evans mechanism. Confirm the actual CurtainsUK kit before public release; spring, ceiling-only, extended and motorised mechanisms need matching instructions.

No automatic bracket count, drilling spacing, screw size, chain length or installation-height calculation is asserted. These depend on the supplied rail, safety mechanism and substrate. Breakaway and separate-tensioner systems are distinguished. No child-safety certification is inferred from this guide.
