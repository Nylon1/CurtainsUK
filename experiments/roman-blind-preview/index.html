Fold calculation & estimates

Uses the conventional odd half-section method, including the bottom half-section. Your 7 cm headrail allowance leaves 133 cm to fold. Stacked: seven half-sections at 19 cm, with three rods. Cascade: the folds grow by 2 cm towards the bottom, starting at 16.4 cm; the bottom flap is 22.4 cm. The 15–20 cm target selects the top fold, not a limit on every cascade layer.

Coverage assumes the headrail allowance is inside the 140 cm window height. Estimates await your workroom check for fabric thickness, hems and hardware. Screen colours may differ from the sample.
