THE ROMAN BLIND GUIDE

The finishing touch.

How to install your Roman blind, one clear step at a time.

Which fittings does this guide show?

Illustrations show a manual Evans Evaglide BSXL headrail with universal swivel-lock brackets (910002). Confirm these match your delivery. Spring, ceiling-only, extension and motorised fittings need their own instructions.

Read the instructions packed with your blind before fitting. This guide explains the sequence; the supplied instructions determine fixings, bracket positions and safety-device setup.
