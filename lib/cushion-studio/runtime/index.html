How to use Cushion Studio Design, edit and place your cushions: open this guide at any time.

1. Design a cushion

  1. Select a cushion number, or use + to add one.
  2. Choose its shape, finished size, fabric, filling and edge finish. Inspect both faces and adjust the pattern placement; for a bolster, inspect the body and ends.
  3. Choose Add to my layout to see your cushion on furniture.

2. Arrange on furniture

  1. Choose your furniture and colours. Select a cushion number to highlight the cushion you are editing. Use Edit this cushion to change its design.
  2. Choose Left, Centre or Right to guide its placement. You can also focus the scene and use the left/right arrow keys. Cushions that do not fit stay in the unplaced tray.
  3. Use Duplicate cushion or Remove to adjust your set. Switch between Room view and The details to check it.

Keep your progress: use Undo for the last change, Save design to keep your work, and Return to a design to reopen it.
