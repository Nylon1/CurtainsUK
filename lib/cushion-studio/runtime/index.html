MAKE IT YOURS

Design a cushion

01
  1. 1

    Pick your cushion

    Select its number, or tap + to add another.

  2. 2

    Make the details yours

    Choose shape, size, fabric, filling and finish. Check both faces and pattern placement, or the body and ends for a bolster.

  3. 3

    See it in your room

    Select Add to my layout to place it on furniture.

BRING IT TOGETHER

Arrange on furniture

02
  1. 1

    Choose the setting

    Pick your furniture and colours. Select a cushion number to highlight it; Edit this cushion changes its design.

  2. 2

    Find its place

    Use Left, Centre or Right. With the scene focused, left/right arrow keys work too. Pieces that do not fit stay in the tray.

  3. 3

    Fine-tune your set

    Duplicate cushion or Remove, then check Room view and The details.
