Fitting references & calculation notes

Each sewn pleat takes one hook. Extra end hooks are counted separately. Every hook needs a compatible hanging eye, including any fixed end eye used instead of a moving glider. Positions follow the sewn pleats; pole width cannot determine their spacing.

Hook height is actual curtain drop minus the vertical distance from the supporting eye to the desired hem. This locates the hook's resting point, not the sharp tip. Trial-fit before repeating.
