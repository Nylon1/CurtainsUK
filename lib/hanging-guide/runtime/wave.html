Fitting references & calculation notes

Hook counts use the Evans EasiWave calculator: curtain coverage ÷ 8 cm + 1.5, rounded up to an even number. Evans' reference flat width is hook count × 16 cm. The first-to-last hook distance is (hook count − 1) × 16 cm.
