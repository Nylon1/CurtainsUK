A LITTLE GUIDANCE, A BEAUTIFUL START

How to use the Hotel Design Studio

Design your hotel rooms. Coordinate your furnishings. Plan your project.

Start with a room, explore its design, then add the details you want CurtainsUK to review. You can work on one room or a whole hotel.

01

Start with your room

Name your project at the top of the studio. Use the room type and scheme selectors to choose what you are working on; your selection carries across the design and planning sections.

Choose Single for a room with one single bed, Standard Double for a double bedroom, or Superior for a more spacious room. These scenes are available in the room-scene selector across the workspace. Saved Projects also lets you choose a starting room for a new project.

For just one bedroom, use one room type with a quantity of 1. For a larger hotel, choose each new room type in Project Planner and add its quantity. “Single” describes the room design; its quantity can be one or many.

Use Duplicate scheme to explore another design, then Compare alternatives to see both choices.

02

Dress the window & set the mood

Choose a room scene in Room Visualiser. Select curtains, voile or both layers, then move the separate sliders to open and close them. The voile sits behind the main curtains.

Select an item, then choose a Panaz fabric or a concept colour. Try the room, curtain-detail and ceiling camera views.

Further down, change wall, ceiling and floor colours, switch the lights on or off, add pictures and choose delicate wall relief or a statement ceiling. Use Preview pinned to keep the image visible as you scroll, or unpin it for more screen space.

03

Coordinate your furnishings

Choose the runner, cushions, headboard, bed base, armchair or bench in Furnishing Coordination. Each item keeps its own selection.

Reuse the same fabric, match another item's colour, or choose a complementary fabric independently. Reusing a choice copies it; later changes to the original item do not automatically change the others.

Check the fabric reference and published intended uses. A visual selection does not confirm suitability for upholstery or a finished furnishing.

04

Plan the space

In Room Layout Planner, enter room width and length in centimetres. Add windows and doors with their positions and dimensions.

Add furniture, then drag it or enter its position and size numerically. Rotate pieces to try different arrangements. Review overlap and boundary warnings, and export the plan when ready.

Template dimensions are examples until you enter and confirm them. Changing the room scene keeps your existing layout. To start again with that scene's example plan, select Use scene template and confirm the replacement.

The 2D plan is separate from the room illustration: moving furniture or changing measurements does not rebuild the 3D scene.

05

Add rooms & measurements

In Project Planner, start with the room type and quantity. Below these, use Add individual room for each bedroom.

Enter its room number, window/opening name, width and drop in centimetres. For another window in the same room, add a record using the same room number. Tick the measurement check only after checking that room and window.

Open Notes or different design for an exception. Shared references, sample statuses and installation notes are optional expandable sections. Grouping rooms does not confirm they have identical measurements.

06

Save, share & prepare an enquiry

Changes save in this browser; check the saved status at the top. Saved Projects lets you reopen or duplicate projects. Download a JSON backup to keep a copy or transfer a project to another device or preview address.

In Enquiry & downloads, add your contact details, export a PDF brief or open Schedules & backup for CSV schedules and a project backup.

Prepare a sample request or quotation enquiry, review the draft, then download it or open your email app. Attach the brief and schedules yourself. The studio does not send enquiries, order samples or book installations; status fields are manual notes.

Before you share your design

Confirm measurements, check room exceptions and review unresolved decisions. Physical samples are needed to assess colour, texture and sheerness. Room images and lighting are illustrative; daylight previews do not certify blackout performance.

Panaz FR-tested fabric range: supplier-published ratings are shown with fabrics. Confirm the intended use, finished construction and project requirements; the studio does not certify the whole room.

Projects are saved only in this browser on this device. Clearing browser data can remove them. Keep a downloaded backup.
