01 / THE FOUNDATION

Position, secure, check.

01

Mark the track position

Measure the entire intended track, including the coverage beyond the glass. Mark its ends and motor side. Use a level to establish the mounting line.

Check your curtain heading and finished drop against the actual rail position. For wave curtains, the heading must run below the rail without rubbing it.

02

Fix the brackets, then the track

Secure the supplied mounting hardware to a suitable surface, following its own fixing instructions. Attach the rail and check every bracket is fully engaged before adding the motor or curtain load.

Standard FMS mounting reference

Forest’s FMS assembly guide, page 12, shows 50 cm between brackets and the first bracket close to the motor pulley. Page 13 shows the track being attached to the clips. This reference applies only where your supplied track and brackets match that FMS system. Confirm different bracket or track arrangements before fitting.

02 / YOUR MOTOR

Fit and initialise.

03

Attach and lock the motor

Leave the mains plug disconnected. Attach the Shuttle Go motor to the installed track and twist it anticlockwise. Slide its locking clip upwards to secure it.

Check that the motor is locked before continuing.

Remove the iOn battery first, releasing its retaining strap if fitted. Leave the curtains off the track and place the master carriers halfway open.

Fit the motor into the pulley, turn it into position and move the locking pin upwards. Remove the battery’s security cap, then insert the battery until it clicks.

04

Set the end positions

  1. Hang the curtains on the installed track.
  2. Move them manually to the open position.
  3. Connect the supplied UK three-pin plug to a suitable socket.
  4. Give the curtains a short pull towards closed.
  5. Allow the motor to close and reopen the curtains automatically. The end positions are now set.

Keep the cable clear of walkways. The motor needs a continuous supply; do not use a hotel-style key-card power cutoff.

Keep the curtains off during limit setup. From halfway open, pull the master carriers towards closed. They travel closed, then return open automatically.

At the open end, the yellow LED4 stays on while stack-back can be adjusted. After 10 seconds without movement, the position is stored. Forest specifies a minimum automatic 3 cm adjustment. Hang the curtains only after the limits are set.

03 / IN YOUR HAND

Meet your EasyTouch remote.

05

Pair a channel

Slide the rear cover upwards. Insert two AAA batteries with the polarity shown inside, then replace the cover. Select an individual channel using the left or right arrow.

Motor P × 3→Hold P: 3 secondsHold P: 4 seconds→Remote Open

After three short presses on the motor’s programming button, hold the fourth press for 3 seconds4 seconds. Press Open on the selected remote channel within 10 seconds of the pairing indicator flashing.

The Go motor jogs twice to confirm pairing.

Use one channel for each room you want to operate independently. You can pair more than one motor to a channel. With all channel lights lit, the remote operates all its active channels together.

Everyday movement

Select the channel, then press Open or Close. Press the square Stop button to pause. Once stopped, choose either direction. Keep the travel path clear and supervise children.

Programming lock

Hold the rear Program button for 10 seconds until the LED flickers once. Repeat to unlock programming. This is a programming lock; ordinary movement controls remain available.

Choose active channels

Hold Channel + and Favourite together for 3 seconds. Use the channel arrows to set the number of active channels, then press Stop. The LED flashes three times to confirm.

Remote response and radio protocols

EasyTouch transmits both original Forest RF and Forest Link by default. This supports the two motor families without adding a Wi-Fi accessory.

If you only use original-protocol motors such as iOn and notice slow response, Forest describes an optional original-RF mode: remove the batteries, hold Stop while reinserting them, and check for one LED flash. Re-pair the motors afterwards. Do not use this as a general step for a mixed Go and iOn installation; consult the remote manual, page 15.

Save or remove a favourite position

With the end positions already set, move to the desired position and stop. Hold Favourite and Stop together for 2 seconds. One favourite is stored per motor. Press Favourite to recall it. To remove it, recall that position, then hold Favourite and Stop for 2 seconds again.

The EasyTouch favourite button requires Forest Link. The documented iOn uses original Forest RF, so this package does not promise that favourite-button function or built-in app control.

Connect Shuttle Go to the real Forest Connect app

Use the official Forest Connect app on your phone and follow its setup flow. The Go has built-in Wi-Fi; no separate Wi-Fi dongle is required. Use a 2.4 GHz network. Press the motor P button twice, then hold the third press for 3 seconds to enter Wi-Fi pairing; its indicator flashes blue.

The website’s interactive demonstration does not pair devices, create a Forest account or change your real motor. See the Go manual, pages 17–18, for the app pairing screens.

Adjustments, unpairing and resets

Use the instructions for your motor only. Resetting limits or factory settings changes a working installation. Keep the path clear and be ready to set the limits again.

04 / KEEP IT RUNNING SMOOTHLY

A few checks, then enjoy.

Final test

Check the track is secure and the motor locked. Check the curtain weight against the selected motor and track limit. Test Open, Stop and Close on the intended channel. The cloth should move freely without rubbing the rail, furniture or window hardware.

Remote not responding?

Check the selected channel, battery polarity and pairing. An orange remote LED warns of low batteries. Replace both AAA batteries. If several rooms move unexpectedly, check whether all channel lights are lit.

Motor stops unexpectedly?

Stop using the track if anything is loose, damaged or obstructed. Check the curtain path and power source. Do not force a jammed curtain or repeatedly run the motor against an obstruction. Use the correct motor troubleshooting manual or contact CurtainsUK.

Recharge the iOn battery

Slow operation can indicate a low battery. Remove the battery and use its Forest charger. Orange means charging; green means fully charged. Allow about 5 hours. Charge indoors in dry conditions at 10°C or warmer, away from direct sunlight. Replace damaged batteries or chargers; do not repair them or substitute an unrelated charger.

Before cleaning or maintenance, disconnect the Go from the mains or remove the iOn battery. Use the manufacturer’s care instructions and approved lubrication method. Do not open the motor, drill it, immerse it or use aggressive cleaning chemicals. Damaged mains cables must be dealt with by a qualified electrician. These products are for dry indoor spaces.
