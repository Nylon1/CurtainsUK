Straight tracks only, up to 500 cm. Enter the finished track length you require. We keep your exact entry.

How to measure your track
  1. Measure the track you need, not just the glass. Allow for intended curtain coverage and available wall space.
  2. Check the mounting position and space for the motor. Confirm clearances from the supplied installation drawing.
  3. For Shuttle Go, check the mains connection. For Shuttle iOn, allow access to remove the battery for charging.
  4. Use the inside-facing-window viewpoint for every left and right choice.
  5. Review the final dimensions before adding your track.

Supply only. Fitting and electrical work are not included.

How to weigh your curtains

Weigh all the panels for this track, including their lining, interlining, headings and hooks. Use suitable scales if you can safely handle the curtains. Alternatively, note your own weight, weigh yourself holding the curtains, then subtract your own reading. Add the results for every panel. Ask for help if the curtains are too heavy or awkward to handle safely.

The headline maximum is 35 kg for Shuttle Go or 25 kg for battery-powered Shuttle iOn on a straight track, subject to the supplied configuration. Movement in this demonstration is not load approval.
