Designed to help you visualise your interior

This visualiser is designed to help you explore how different curtain fabrics could work with your walls, flooring and furniture colours. The visualiser uses available fabric and pattern information to create a representative view, but it is not a photograph or exact prediction of your finished curtains. Actual appearance will vary according to window size, curtain dimensions, heading, fullness, lining, fabric characteristics and finishing details. Seams, hems and stitching may not be shown.

Always use a physical fabric sample when making your final colour and fabric choice.
